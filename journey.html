Our Journey

Learning Experiences

At FARMEASY, our journey has been one of innovation, collaboration, and growth. From initial ideation to prototyping and market testing, we've gained invaluable insights that shape our approach to revolutionizing agriculture in Nigeria.


  1. Team Collaboration: The ability to communicate and work effectively with others toward common goals, fostering a supportive environment for creativity and problem-solving.
  2. Business Acumen: Understanding market validation, business models, and target markets for successful entrepreneurship, ensuring our solutions meet real-world needs.
  3. Prototyping Processes: Methods for designing and testing prototypes to turn ideas into tangible products, refining our SoftSpray technology through iterative development.

Key Milestones

  • 2023 - Ideation Phase: Brainstormed and conceptualized the SoftSpray device to address inefficiencies in traditional spraying methods.
  • 2024 - Prototyping: Developed initial prototypes, incorporating feedback from farmers and agricultural experts.
  • 2025 - Launch: Officially launched FARMEASY, aiming to empower farmers with sustainable and efficient tools.

Our journey continues as we expand our impact, driven by a commitment to innovation and community.

Collaborations and Support

FARMEASY has been supported by key partners who share our vision for transforming agriculture in Nigeria. We collaborate with institutions and organizations to drive innovation and provide resources for our projects.


  • Innov8: Providing mentorship and resources for entrepreneurial development.
  • Osun State University: Academic collaboration for research and development.
  • TETFUND: Funding support for educational and research initiatives.
